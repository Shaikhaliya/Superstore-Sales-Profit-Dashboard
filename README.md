# Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
An interactive Excel dashboard built to analyze sales performance, profitability, product categories, customer segments, regions, and shipping methods. The project transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights using Excel data analysis and visualization features.

## Tools & Skills
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Interactive Slicers
- Data Cleaning and Formatting
- KPI Cards
- Data Visualization
- Business Insights and Reporting

## Dashboard Features
- **KPI Cards:** Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin, and Total Records
- **Category Analysis:** Sales and profit comparison across product categories
- **Regional Analysis:** Profit comparison across regions
- **Monthly Sales Trends:** Identify high- and low-performing months
- **Customer Segments:** Analyze sales contribution by customer segment
- **Product Analysis:** Top products by sales and sub-categories by profit
- **Shipping Analysis:** Compare sales across shipping modes
- **Interactive Filters:** Region, Segment, Category, and Order Year slicers

## Key Business Insights
- Technology recorded the highest sales among the three product categories.
- The West region generated the highest regional profit.
- November recorded the highest monthly sales in the monthly summary.
- Consumer customers contributed approximately 50% of total sales.
- The dashboard helps compare sales and profitability across different business dimensions.

## How to Use
1. Download the Excel workbook from this repository.
2. Open it in Microsoft Excel.
3. Navigate to the **Dashboard** sheet.
4. Use the slicers to filter the analysis by region, customer segment, category, and order year.
5. Explore the charts and KPIs to identify business trends.
